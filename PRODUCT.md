# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
High-end creative agencies looking for top-tier talent who can blend traditional video editing with cutting-edge technology.

## Product Purpose
To represent Suresh Vizhal as a premier candidate by showcasing a mastery of both cinematic storytelling (Premiere/After Effects) and programmatic video engineering (HyperFrames/Remotion/AI).

## Brand Personality
Masterful, Futuristic, Premium, "God Mode", Technical. The tone is confident and slightly robotic but with an undeniable human craft behind it.

## Anti-references
Generic website walkthroughs, cheap "drag-and-drop" portfolio templates, static/flat designs, over-explaining simple concepts.

## Design Principles
1. **Code as Craft**: Explicitly showcase the programmatic pipeline (JSON -> Code -> Video) as a creative superpower.
2. **Cinematic Authority**: Utilize scale, high-contrast typography, and purposeful motion to command attention like a professional film intro.
3. **Expert Confidence**: Present the "God Mode" complexity with clarity, allowing the masterful execution to build trust with high-end agencies.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Maintain high contrast for readability, provide clear focus states, and ensure motion can be reduced if the user has system preferences set, without losing the brand's premium feel.
