# PRODUCT.md: Visual Architect Portfolio

## Product Overview
Visual Architect is a high-fidelity, "NLE Workspace" (Non-Linear Editor) themed portfolio for Suresh Vizhal V R, a Senior Video Editor and Motion Graphics Artist. The website is not just a gallery; it's a technical instrument built in code that mimics the professional environments of tools like Adobe Premiere Pro, After Effects, and DaVinci Resolve.

## Users
- **Creative Directors & Producers:** Looking for high-end video editing talent with technical precision.
- **Marketing Managers:** Seeking editors who understand brand narratives and AI-assisted content creation.
- **Tech-Savvy Recruits:** Who appreciate the intersection of code, design, and video production.

## Brand & Tone
- **Precision:** Every element should feel like it has a technical purpose.
- **Cinematic:** High-contrast, dark mode, moody but clear.
- **Instrumental:** The UI should feel like a "pro tool" rather than a "marketing site."
- **Innovative:** Seamless integration of AI-assisted themes and code-based visuals.

## Strategic Principles
1. **The NLE Aesthetic:** Use UI metaphors from video editing software (timelines, monitors, property inspectors).
2. **Technical Density:** Show metadata and technical readouts (Resolution, FPS, Bitrate) to reinforce professional authority.
3. **Motion-First:** Use subtle, purposeful animations that mimic technical feedback (scanlines, focus locks, system pulses).
